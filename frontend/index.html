Section 5

Scientific Limitations & Safety Disclaimers

Essential operational caveats, modeling boundaries, and safety constraints governing this research system.

🏛️

Research Prototype Status

This system is an academic research demonstration, not an operational public meteorological alerting service. It must never be used as a standalone source for life-safety warnings, evacuations, or aviation planning. Official alerts from the India Meteorological Department (IMD) take legal and operational precedence.

📅

No Prospective Post-2023 Test

The model was trained on 2019–2021, calibrated on 2022, and evaluated retrospectively on 2023. Because 2023 results have been inspected during research iterations, 2023 serves as development evidence. Rigorous prospective validation on live post-2023 data remains future work.

🎯

Spatial Scale & Localization Limits

Exact 0.25° grid-cell localization of rare extreme convective cells remains challenging (exact-cell Heavy F1 is ~0.057). Practical operational skill exists within 50–100 km regional radii (F1 reaches 0.443–0.598), reflecting meso-scale thunderstorm cluster predictability.

🔄

Overlapping Regional Mass vs Stroke Counts

The prediction target (expected_region50_count) measures overlapping 50 km regional convective severity. Summing this metric across grid cells yields regional mass, not a count of isolated lightning strikes across India. Comparing it against discrete stroke counts is scientifically invalid.

📈

Recursive Uncertainty Compounding

The current 24-hour candidate recursively substitutes model-predicted regional flash densities into lag features at +12h, +18h, and +24h. Errors and biases compound with each horizon step. Early horizons (+6h) exhibit significantly higher reliability than later horizons (+24h).

📡

Data Latency & Sensor Dependency

Real-time skill strictly depends on continuous, low-latency lightning sensor observations (WWLLN / satellite networks). In the absence of a live seed feed, forecasts operate in time-rebased demonstration mode and do not reflect current atmospheric conditions.

Scientific Assessment Verdict:

"The system demonstrates useful six-hour regional lightning-risk localization, particularly when evaluated within operationally meaningful 50–100 km spatial tolerances. Exact-cell prediction and rare-event severity remain research limitations. The 2023 comparison is chronological development evidence, while the current +24-hour recursive product is a demonstration that requires a timely lightning-history feed and prospective validation before operational use."
